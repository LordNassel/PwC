Amazon.com, Inc. Q1 2024 Financial Results

Amazon.com, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMZN) today announced financial results for its first quarter ended March 31, 2024.

Financial Highlights:
- Net sales increased 13% to 143300 million dollars in the first quarter, compared with 127400 million dollars in first quarter 2023.
- North America segment sales increased 12% year-over-year to 86300 million dollars.
- International segment sales increased 10% year-over-year to 31900 million dollars.
- AWS (Amazon Web Services) segment sales increased 17% year-over-year to 25000 million dollars.

Operating income increased to 15300 million dollars in the first quarter, compared with 4800 million dollars in first quarter 2023.
- North America segment operating income was 5000 million dollars.
- International segment operating income was 900 million dollars.
- AWS operating income was 9400 million dollars.

Net income increased to 10400 million dollars in the first quarter, or 1.00 dollar per diluted share, compared with 3200 million dollars, or 0.31 dollars per diluted share, in first quarter 2023.

"It was a good start to the year across the business, and you can see that in both our customer experience improvements and financial results," said Andy Jassy, Amazon President and CEO.
