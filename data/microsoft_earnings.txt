MICROSOFT CORPORATION
ANNUAL FISCAL REVENUE REPORT

BUSINESS OVERVIEW:
Microsoft Corporation continued its aggressive enterprise expansion, powered primarily by structural growth in hyper-scale cloud computational deployments, hybrid workplace tooling setups, and commercial software licensing agreements.

CONSOLIDATED TOTAL REVENUE SUMMARY:
- Microsoft total revenue was 143000 million dollars in 2020. The onset of enterprise operational digitizations caused a surge in remote productivity demands and commercial server updates.
- Microsoft total revenue was 168000 million dollars in 2021. Growth accelerated across the commercial cloud portfolio, anchored firmly by Azure infrastructure expansions and wider adoption of enterprise security tiers.

SEGMENT REVENUE NOTES:
Intelligent Cloud revenue grew rapidly, while the Personal Computing segment stayed resilient due to steady licensing demand and Xbox ecosystem content engagement.
